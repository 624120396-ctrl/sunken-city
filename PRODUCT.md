# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sunken City serves CoC tabletop users across two main contexts: KP and administrators on PC need dense, reliable management surfaces; PL players on mobile and PC need quick room entry, readable chat, dice records, character state, and lightweight community participation.

## Product Purpose

The product provides an immersive web platform for CoC campaign play, character management, story rooms, economy systems, and community discussion. Success means users can stay in the fiction while still reading, scanning, and acting without UI friction.

## Brand Personality

Luminous occult archive: deep-sea mist, readable smoked glass, restrained ritual gold, selective blood-red danger, and a non-pure-black Cthulhu atmosphere.

## Anti-references

Avoid global black veils as a readability crutch, gray-on-gray text, low-opacity gold body copy, large blood-red decoration, mobile layouts that bury the primary task, and page-specific overlay patches that do not strengthen the shared UI system.

## Design Principles

1. Backgrounds provide atmosphere; surfaces provide readability.
2. PC surfaces can be immersive and dense, but must remain scannable for KP and admin workflows.
3. Mobile prioritizes participation: room chat, dice records, state, then tools.
4. Community and long-form surfaces should feel like archive records, not decorative cards.
5. Shared components and tokens should carry visual decisions so pages do not drift.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast for primary and secondary text on bright, balanced, and dark backgrounds. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep interactive targets usable on touch screens, and never convey critical state through color alone.
